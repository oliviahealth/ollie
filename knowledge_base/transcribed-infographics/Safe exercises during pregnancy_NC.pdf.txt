SAFE
EXERCISES

TO TRY DURING
PREGNANCY

Walking

Engages many muscles and joints
in a low-impact, full-body
movement.

Yoga

You can find designated yoga

classes for pregnant women!
Yoga can help with stress,
stretching, and breathing.

Swimming
Swimming is a low-impact
exercise that supports your
body weight, reducing joint
strain and injury risk during

pregnancy.

Stationary Bicycling
The unequal weight of your
belly may make normal
biking dangerous, but using
a stationary bike is a great
way to get the same
workout in!
