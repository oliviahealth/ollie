Skin-to-skin contact offers many benefits for
babies. Learn more below.

WHAT IS IT?

Skin-to-skin contact is

when a parent holds
their baby, who is
wearing only a diaper,
directly against the
parent’s bare chest. This
can be done by either
moms or dads.

BENEFITS

Helps regulate a
newborn's body
temperature

Promotes better sleep in
newborns

Supports healthy newborn
weight gain

Stabilizes heart rate and
oxygen levels, supporting
brain development
Increases breastfeeding
success

Strengthens the
newborn’s immune
system

Enhances parent-baby
bonding

Reduces the risk of
postpartum depression

HOW TO PRACTICE
SKIN-TO-SKIN

Skin-to-skin is beneficial at any time during infancy,
but the first 2 hours after birth are the most

Sit in a quiet, private room. Place your diapered baby
on your bare chest for twenty minutes or more. You
can cover up with a blanket, as long as baby’s skin is
in direct contact with their parent's skin.

You can experiment to find what works best for you
and your baby. Continue skin-to-skin as long as it is
comfortable and enjoyable for both of you. If your
baby becomes fussy, it’s perfectly okay to stop.
