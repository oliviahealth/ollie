Tracking Ovulation

A menstrual cycle is measured from the first day of
menstruation to the first day of the next cycle. Typically, a
cycle lasts 28-32 days, though some women may have
shorter or longer cycles. Ovulation usually occurs between
days 11 and 21, which is called the “fertile window.”
