Using Your Last Period

Pregnancy typically lasts about 40
weeks. To estimate your due date,
count 40 weeks (or 280 days) from the
first day of your last menstrual period.
This is the most common method
healthcare providers use to calculate a
due date.

Using Your IVF Transfer Date

Embryo transfers usually happen three or five days

Your due date can change for several reasons. This may
happen if you have irregular periods, if a later ultrasound
provides new information, or for other medical reasons.

If you have any concerns, contact your provider. They
can answer your questions and provide guidance based
on your specific situation.
