TYPES OF ADOPTION

¢ Open Adoption: Birth parents and
adoptive families are able to
communicate and build a relationship.
This may include emails, letters,
phone calls, or in-person visits
¢ Semi-Open Adoption: Identifying
information is kept confidential.
Communication is typically shared
through an agency and may include
letters or cards
Closed Adoption: There is no contact
or exchange of identifying
information between birth parents
and adoptive families

HOW MUCH WILL IT COST ME?

As a birth parent, you typically do not
have to pay any adoption-related costs.
In most cases, the adoptive family
covers medical expenses and agency or
legal fees. For specific details, discuss
the financial arrangements with the
adoption agency you choose.
