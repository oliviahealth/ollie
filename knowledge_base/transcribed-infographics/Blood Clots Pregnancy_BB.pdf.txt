BLOOD CLOTS DURING

Pregnancy

WHAT IS A BLOOD CLOT?

A BLOOD CLOT IS A CLUMP OF BLOOD THAT FORMS TO HELP
STOP BLEEDING IN THE BODY. HOWEVER, IF A CLOT BLOCKS
BLOOD FLOW AND PREVENTS OXYGEN FROM REACHING
TISSUES, IT CAN BECOME A CONCERN.

WHO CAN GET BLOOD CLOTS?

ANYONE CAN DEVELOP A BLOOD CLOT, BUT PREGNANT
WOMEN AND THOSE WHO HAVE RECENTLY GIVEN BIRTH ARE
AT A HIGHER RISK. IF YOU HAVE A FAMILY HISTORY OF
BLOOD CLOTS OR BLEEDING DISORDERS, BE SURE TO INFORM
YOUR PROVIDER DURING YOUR PRENATAL APPOINTMENTS.

WHY ARE PREGNANT WOMEN AT A HIGHER RISK?

PREGNANCY CAUSES NATURAL CHANGES IN THE BODY THAT
INCREASE THE LIKELIHOOD OF BLOOD CLOTTING. FOR EXAMPLE,
THE BODY BECOMES MORE PRONE TO CLOTTING IN
PREPARATION FOR POTENTIAL BLEEDING DURING DELIVERY.
PREGNANCY MAY ALSO LEAD TO DECREASED PHYSICAL
ACTIVITY, WHICH CAN FURTHER RAISE THE RISK. ADDITIONAL
RISK FACTORS INCLUDE HIGH BLOOD PRESSURE, OBESITY,
SMOKING, PROLONGED IMMOBILITY, AND DEHYDRATION.

COMPLICATIONS OF CLOTS DURING PREGNANCY

BLOOD CLOTS DURING PREGNANCY CAN LEAD TO SERIOUS
COMPLICATIONS, INCLUDING HEART ATTACK, CLOTS IN THE
PLACENTA, PREECLAMPSIA, PULMONARY EMBOLISM, PRETERM
BIRTH, AND STROKE. BECAUSE OF THESE POTENTIAL RISKS,
TAKING PREVENTIVE MEASURES IS ESSENTIAL TO HELP REDUCE
THE LIKELIHOOD OF CLOT FORMATION.

TIPS TO REDUCE THE RISK OF BLOOD CLOTS

STAY WELL HYDRATED

MOVE REGULARLY BY WALKING AND STRETCHING
LEARN THE WARNING SIGNS OF A BLOOD CLOT AND
MONITOR FOR SYMPTOMS

WEAR COMPRESSION STOCKINGS IF RECOMMENDED
DISCUSS ANY CONCERNS ABOUT BLOOD CLOTS WITH
YOUR PROVIDER

CONTACT YOUR PROVIDER WITH
QUESTIONS OR CONCERNS.
