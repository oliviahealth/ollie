EXERCISE

Physical activity is typically
safe during pregnancy and can
make a huge difference.
However, talk to your provider
before engaging in exercise if
you have pregnancy
complications.

SLEEP

Getting enough rest is important
for managing anxiety. Consider
using pregnancy pillows, calming
lotion, or other comfort measures
to create a relaxing bedtime
routine.

MINDFULNESS

Practicing mindfulness and
similar techniques can help
relieve anxiety about labor,
postpartum challenges, or
other stressors.

RELAXATION
STRATEGIES:

Try yoga, massages, deep
breathing, or calming
hobbies to help you unwind.

JOURNALING

Writing down thoughts ina
journal allows you to sort
through your feelings, think
of solutions, and put the
worries from your head
directly onto paper.
