CHECKUP BEFORE PREGNANCY

PRECONCEPTION
CHECKUP

WHAT IS IT?

A preconception checkup is a checkup done
before pregnancy to determine if you can have
a healthy pregnancy. The provider will look for

any conditions that may affect pregnancy,
such as high blood pressure or diabetes. This
=, { _ checkup can be done by an obstetrician, family

doctor, family nurse practitioner, or a certified
midwife.

WHEN SHOULD I HAVE ONE?

You should get one before each pregnancy if
you Nave the chance. You can do it at any time
within a year before getting pregnant. It is
especially important to get one if you have of
previously experienced a premature birth,
miscarriage, stillbirth, or had a baby with birth
defects. Your provider may be able to help you
avoid experiencing this again.

WHAT HAPPENS THERE?

Your provider will check your overall
health. You may talk about health
conditions that might affect pregnancy,
medications you take, supplements you
may need, vaccines, quitting smoking
and drinking, and more! Exams and
testing may be done.

EXAMS

Your provier may do a series of
exams on you. Some of these may
include a full physical exam anda
pelvic exam. These exams will give
the provider more insight into the

health of your body.

TESTING

Some tests may be run. Possible
tests include a Pap smear to check
for cervical cancer, blood typing,
or genetic testing. Genetic testing
may be done to determine the risk
for genetic defects in your baby.
