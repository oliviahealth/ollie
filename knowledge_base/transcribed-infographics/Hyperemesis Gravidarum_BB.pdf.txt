HYPEREMESIS
GRAVIDARUM

Nausea in the first trimester of pregnancy, known as
morning sickness, is acommon pregnancy symptom. It is a
normal part of pregnancy. However, it can become an issue

when the sickness becomes excessive.

WHAT IS IT?

Hyperemesis gravidarum isa condition where nausea and
vomiting get so serious that a woman loses weight and becomes
severely dehydrated. This is thought to be caused by a rise in
hormone levels. Symptoms usually occur between 4-6 weeks
and may peak at 9-13 weeks. It may resolve after that, or it may
continue throughout the pregnancy.

SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS

e Severe nausea and vomiting e Increased heart rate

e Food aversions e Extreme fatigue

e Headaches e Weight loss

e Fainting e Inability to keep any
e Dehydration food or drinks down

e Confusion
Low blood pressure

POTENTIAL
PROBLEMS

This condition should be treated promptly. Because of
severe nausea and vomiting, the mom is unable to meet
her and her baby’s nutritional needs. This can affect the
baby’s growth and development. Mom will also suffer
from dehydration and electrolyte imbalances.

TREATMENT OPTIONS

e Eat dry crackersin the morning e Small, frequent meals
e Bland diet e No spicy/fatty food

e Intravenous fluids e Avoid triggers (smells)
e Tube feeding e High protein snacks

e Medications e Drink lots of fluids

e Bed rest when not nauseous

e Herbs (ginger and peppermint) e Speak with your
provider about options
