What is Rh Factor?

Rh factor is a protein that is found on the
surface of your red blood cells. Some people
have it and some do not. If you do have it, you
are Rh positive. If not, you are Rh negative.
Most people are positive. This is what the + or -
at the end of your blood type indicates.

What is Rh Incompatibility?

This is when a pregnant woman is Rh negative but her baby
is Rh positive. If your baby's blood comes into contact with
yours, your body will recognize Rh as a foreign object and
will build antibodies against it. This can lead to your immune
system attacking the Rh positive red blood cells in your
current baby or future Rh positive babies.

RhoGAM

This is the injection that prevents Rh
incompatability during pregnancy. It contains
antibodies that stop your immune system from
reacting to your baby's Rh factor.

It is injected into your arm or buttocks.
