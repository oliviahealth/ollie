How Much Does It Cost to
Have a Baby?

HOW MUCH DOES IT COST TO GIVE BIRTH?
In July 2022, it was estimated that the health costs
associated with pregnancy and childbirth come out to be
about $19,000. Health insurance should cover a good
portion of this, leaving new parents paying about $3,000 out
of pocket.

HOW MUCH DOES IT COST TO HAVE A BABY?

On average, a middle-income family may spend about
$18,000 per year on their child, not including the cost
of childbirth. Many families report spending around

27% of their income on expenses related to their baby.

HOW TO SAVE UP FOR A BABY

Start small and take it one step at a time. Aim to set
aside money for pregnancy, delivery, and living
expenses for the first few months.

Look for simple ways to cut back on spending and put
those savings toward your baby.
