WAYS TO REDUCE THE RISK OF SIDS

e Place babies on their backs for naps and bedtime

e Ensure that the baby’s sleep surface is firm (returns to its
original shape quickly if pressed on), flat (like a table, not a
hammock), leveled (not at an angle or incline), and
covered only with a fitted sheet

e Keep things OUT of the baby’s sleeping area-no soft
objects or toys

e Stay smoke and vape free during pregnancy, and keep
baby’s surroundings free of smoke

e Stay drug and alcohol free during pregnancy

e Get regular medical care throughout pregnancy

¢ Give baby plenty of “tummy time” when they are awake
